How the online eye test works

The check guides you through consent, accessibility settings, setup, calibration, a quick practice step, and a separate test for each eye.

Back to home Start on the home page

1. Safety and consent

You confirm that this is a screening check and review urgent red-flag symptoms first.

2. Accessibility setup

Choose text size, contrast, audio guidance, and your preferred answer method.

3. Guided setup and calibration

Stable lighting, reduced glare, and optional calibration help make the result more reliable.

  1. Practice
    Try one sample item so you know how the answer step works.
  2. Test each eye separately
    The app covers one eye at a time and records each side on its own.
  3. Review the result
    You receive a simple summary with confidence notes and next steps.
Important: Results can change with distance, glare, lighting, and how steady your setup is. This page explains the flow, but it is not a substitute for a full eye examination.
