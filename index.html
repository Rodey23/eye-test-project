Before you start

This is a screening check — not a diagnosis.

Stop and seek urgent advice if you have red‑flag symptoms
  • Sudden flashes of light or new floaters
  • A curtain/shadow across your vision
  • Severe eye pain or headache
  • Sudden loss of vision
  • Eye injury or chemical exposure

Cover one eye safely

  • Do not press on your eyelid
  • Keep both eyes open behind the cover

Limitations

This is a screening check, not a diagnosis. Results can vary with distance, glare, lighting and user setup. Visual acuity alone cannot detect all eye disease. Seek urgent care for red‑flag symptoms.

Recommended next steps

  • If results suggest reduced vision, book an optometrist appointment.
  • If you have flashes/floaters, curtain shadow, severe pain, sudden loss of vision, or injury — seek urgent care.
  • Consider a full eye exam every 1–2 years.
