Privacy Local-first design

Privacy and data handling

The site is designed so you can use the screening check without creating an account or entering personal details.

Back to home Accessibility page

What the site keeps

  • Language choice and accessibility preferences during the session.
  • Screening progress and result state inside the browser session.
  • Anything you choose to export or print yourself.

What the site does not require

  • No name, email, or account is required.
  • No login is needed to start the check.
  • No personal profile is needed to view the result.

Practical privacy notes

If you close the tab, browser storage or session data may be cleared depending on your browser settings. If you export a summary, that file is saved by you on your device and can be shared only if you choose to share it.

Reminder: Privacy does not replace safety. If you have flashes, floaters, a curtain shadow, severe pain, sudden loss of vision, or injury, seek urgent care.
